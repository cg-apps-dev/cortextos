# Card-identifier convention for NEW producers (onboarding insert)
Written 2026-07-25. Applies when KU Creatives / the incoming AI Video Producers start producing cards.

## THE RULE
In any cross-agent or cross-person reference to a card, use the FULL card name including the writer prefix:
  MB_CG_IMG_535   not   IMG_535
Alternative acceptable fix: confirm the IMG/VID numbering is GLOBALLY sequential (assigned centrally, never reused per-writer). If that is guaranteed, bare IMG_535 is safe. Pick one and state which.

## WHY THIS EXISTS (do not delete - this is the part that gets lost)
The short form IMG_535 is currently unambiguous, but ONLY BY ACCIDENT: as of 2026-07-25 one writer effectively owns the static numbering space (checked: MB 393 cards, LB 1, zero collisions). That is a property of STAFFING, not of the naming convention. It ends the moment a second producer numbers into the same space - which is exactly what onboarding new producers does.

When it breaks, it breaks in the worst available way:
1. INVISIBLE WHILE SILENT. Every reader silently expands the shorthand to whichever card they had in mind. The ambiguity never reaches conscious attention, so nobody reports it. Care does not help - the mental repair happens upstream of attention.
2. MIS-ATTRIBUTED WHEN LOUD. When it finally surfaces, it surfaces as two people DISAGREEING ABOUT A CARD'S STATUS. Everyone then debugs statuses, queues and records - and nobody examines the identifier, because the identifier is the one thing both parties are certain they agree on. Effort goes to the wrong place.

You cannot debug your way to this after the fact; by the time it presents, the evidence points elsewhere. That is why the convention is set at onboarding rather than fixed on discovery.
